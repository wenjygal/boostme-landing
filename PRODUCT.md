## Platform

web

## Users

Owners of small/solo Israeli businesses who do everything themselves, and managers in small companies with similar teams. Hebrew speakers. They arrive feeling three things at once:
- Overloaded: "אין לי זמן לזה. מתי אני אמור ללמוד עוד כלי?"
- Tech-confused: "כולם מדברים על AI. לא יודע מה רלוונטי אליי, ומפחד להתחיל עם הדבר הלא נכון."
- Burned by past solutions: "כבר שילמתי על מערכת/יועץ ש'הבטיח הכל'. בסוף אני עדיין עושה הכל לבד."
Moment before contacting: "כולם עושים את זה, אני צריך להדביק, אבל לא יודע מאיפה להתחיל."

## Product Purpose

boost-me.digital is the BOOST ME brand homepage. It presents four equal boosts and converts a visitor into a WhatsApp conversation that opens a free diagnosis call. Success = a visitor recognizes their own pain in one boost and writes on WhatsApp.

## Positioning

BOOST ME finds the right, precise tool for one specific pain, not the biggest project. Against "פתרון מקיף". Promise: one tool used at 100% instead of an expensive system used at 10%. Sometimes the right answer is a short private lesson on a tool the client already owns, and BOOST ME will say "don't buy this".

## Operating Context

Method: diagnosis (find the one real pain) → choice (the precise boost, not the biggest) → build → handover + training. Every boost is quoted separately with its own terms and timeline; no uniform package. Contact happens on WhatsApp (+972545538883).

## Capabilities and Constraints

Four boosts, presented as equals:
- בוסט נחיתה: an offer with no page of its own
- בוסט אוטומציה: tools that don't talk to each other, someone copies by hand
- בוסט עובד דיגיטלי: a bot that answers nicely but not accurately; built on the client's real data
- הדרכה וסדנה (או שיעור פרטי): where to start with AI; Zoom, team / owner / 1:1
Additional services (secondary): audits for existing landing pages/bots, apps (CRM, dashboards, lead handling), measurement setup (GTM, GA4, Clarity), positioning and brand, interactive questionnaires, campaign copy, SEO/AEO/GEO.

Pricing: no numbers on the page. The first step is a free diagnosis call with no commitment.
No fixed timeline promise anywhere (explicit decision).
Hosting: GitHub Pages, custom domain boost-me.digital, GTM container GTM-TVLCVNTV.
Legal: accessibility statement, privacy policy, terms of use (popups) must remain; any change to services/scope/policy requires reviewing the terms of use.

## Product Principles

1. The visitor's pain leads; the service name follows.
2. Small and precise beats comprehensive, in the offer and on the page itself.
3. Honesty over persuasion: no invented proof, no fake urgency, say what you don't need.
4. One next step: WhatsApp for a free diagnosis call.
5. Personal experience before advice: proof comes from things actually built and tried.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Israeli accessibility law: ת"י 5568 / WCAG AA. Hebrew RTL, lang="he". Keyboard navigation, visible focus, ARIA labels on icon/WhatsApp buttons, reduced-motion respected.
